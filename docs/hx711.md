# HX711 bring-up

## Wiring

Target: Waveshare ESP32-S3-Touch-LCD-3.5 (ST7796), not 3.5B. Power off before wiring.

| HX711 module | Waveshare header label |
| --- | --- |
| VCC | 3V3, for a module supporting 3.3 V operation |
| GND | GND |
| DT / DOUT | IO17 |
| SCK / CLK | IO18 |

The GPIO numbers are not physical header positions. GPIO17/18 also serve camera VSYNC/HREF: leave the camera disconnected and disabled. Do not feed 5 V logic to an ESP32 input. Modules with separate analog VCC and digital VDD need their own schematic checked; keep digital VDD at 3.3 V. The load cell connects to E+/E− and A+/A−; B is unused. Red E+, black E−, green A+, yellow A− is only a provisional color mapping for the user's four-wire sensor, not a universal standard. Confirm the cell's datasheet; yellow may instead be a shield.

Pin assignments live in `main/config.hxx`. The protocol is a dedicated clock/data interface, not I2C. Channel A gain 128 uses 24 data clocks plus one selection pulse. Prefer the module's 10 SPS RATE setting for this first implementation. The polling UI loop consumes at most about 10 samples/s even if the module is configured for 80 SPS.

## Calibration and weighing

1. Mount the cell correctly and keep the platform clear. Power the board via USB.
2. The initial display shows `Calibration required` once the sensor responds; it never treats ADC counts as grams.
3. Tap **Calibrate**, let the reading settle, and tap **Capture empty zero**.
4. Place an accurate 100 g, 500 g, or 1000 g reference within the load cell's rated capacity. Wait for all ten samples to settle, then tap the corresponding mass. Never choose a nominal mass that differs from the actual reference.
5. Calibration saves to NVS. Remove the reference, close the dialog, and verify zero and at least one other known mass. Reboot and repeat the check. An explicit `Calibration not saved` message means the in-memory factor is usable only until reboot; repeat calibration after fixing storage.
6. For ingredient weighing, put a container on the platform, wait for **Stable**, then tap **Tare**. Tare does not rewrite the empty-platform reference in NVS.
7. For brewing, put the empty vessel (or prepared pour-over assembly) on the platform and set the dose. Tap **Start brew** before liquid arrives. Start captures a new tare and starts the timer; it does not detect a pour automatically. Finish records the result in RAM. Reset/Next waits for load removal and clears the temporary tare, not calibration.

Register equipment from the Object card. The reference step now captures a stable, calibrated gross measurement rather than the demo's 156.8 g constant. Selection is manual; selecting a profile subtracts its saved empty mass. Live mode starts with no fictional equipment. Profiles and brew history do not survive reboot.

## Corner Cases & Failure Modes

- DOUT high or disconnected: no busy wait; after 1 second without a sample, show `?` and disable tare/start. An input pull-up handles an unconnected DOUT. A DOUT stuck low is rejected if it does not release after the 25th pulse.
- ADC rail values or calibrated magnitude above the software display guard of 10,000 g: show overload/unavailable. This software guard is **not** the cell's safe mechanical capacity. Do not overload your cell.
- Reverse polarity: signed 24-bit decoding and a negative calibration factor are supported.
- Calibration needs ten consecutive samples within a raw range of 500 counts before first calibration; after calibration the range is max(20 counts, 0.3 g equivalent). Tare and Start require the same stable window. These are bring-up thresholds, not an accuracy guarantee. Increase settling time or improve mounting/noise before changing thresholds.
- Calibration rejects zero/negative/nonfinite reference masses and changes below 1000 counts. A disconnect cancels a captured zero. Closing the dialog cancels an unfinished calibration without changing the saved factor.
- During brewing, sensor failure or a net decrease greater than 5 g in one update latches uncertainty. Stable gross mass at or below 2 g is treated as removal. Slow/partial removal is not guaranteed to be detected; inspect the result and reset after disturbance. No automatic restart occurs on reconnect.
- Ten-sample filtering introduces roughly 0.5–1 s of delay at the effective polling rate. Flow is a filtered weight derivative, not a calibrated volumetric flow measurement.
- NVS failures are reported, not “repaired” by erasing the partition. Calibration uses one versioned blob and one commit; temporary tare is never persisted. Recalibrate if the cell, gain, supply, mechanics, or wiring changes.

## Adaptation and validation

Adapted from user-supplied `smart_scale.bak(1).tgz`: `main/hx711.hxx` and `main/scale.cxx` supplied the signed conversion, gain-selection, factor, and separate tare/reference concepts. The old GPIO14/32 assignment, fixed `{866, -78000}` calibration, task delays inside critical sections, unbounded ready loop, and old OLED stack were not imported.

`make test` runs the existing session suite plus signed conversion, negative calibration, tare versus gross, invalid calibration, timeout/reconnect, saturation, sensor removal, and live-session tests. `make preview` also exercises touch calibration, tare, Start, and sensor failure through the actual LVGL renderer using injected raw readings.

Before relying on weight: verify SCK timing with a logic analyzer, calibrate and test multiple reference masses across the working range, repeat tare/reboot, unplug the HX711 during a brew, and verify touch responsiveness and load-removal behavior on the actual board. No physical board is attached to automated tests.
